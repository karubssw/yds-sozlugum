# abandon

**English Definition:** To leave someone or something behind permanently; to give up or forsake completely.

**Türkçe Anlamı:** Terk etmek, bırakmak, vazgeçmek.

**Örnek Cümle (YDS):** The government's decision to abandon the infrastructure project left thousands of workers unemployed and raised serious questions about long-term economic planning.

**Eş Anlamlıları (Synonyms):**
* forsake
* desert
* leave
* relinquish

**Zıt Anlamlıları (Antonyms):**
* retain
* maintain
* uphold
* preserve
