# ability

**English Definition:** The natural, developed, or acquired power or skill of doing something; capacity or talent.

**Türkçe Anlamı:** Yetenek, kapasite, yetki, beceri.

**Örnek Cümle (YDS):** Emotional intelligence, the ability to understand and manage one's emotions, has been proven to be a stronger predictor of professional success than traditional IQ scores.

**Eş Anlamlıları (Synonyms):**
* capability
* capacity
* proficiency
* competence

**Zıt Anlamlıları (Antonyms):**
* inability
* incompetence
* incapacity
* inadequacy
